Long distance care package ideas: 40 things to send that feel like you

All guidesMagpie

A care package is the closest thing to showing up at their door. The best ones aren't expensive: they're full of things that feel like you and things they can use on the days they miss you most.

Things that feel like you

  1. A T-shirt you've worn (yes, it should smell like you)
  2. Your perfume on a scarf or a letter
  3. A photo strip of the two of you
  4. A voice message on a recordable card
  5. A list of inside jokes
  6. A drawing of your favourite memory together

Comfort for bad days

  1. Their favourite tea or hot chocolate
  2. Fuzzy socks
  3. A candle with a calming scent
  4. A face mask and lip balm
  5. A sleep mask and a playlist for falling asleep
  6. A small blanket
  7. A stress ball with a message on it

A taste of your city

  1. Snacks they can't buy where they live
  2. A local specialty with the story behind it
  3. A postcard of your street
  4. A map with the places you'll take them
  5. Coffee from your favourite café

Things to do

  1. A puzzle
  2. A deck of conversation cards
  3. A book with your notes in the margins
  4. Seeds to grow the same plant as you
  5. A disposable camera to fill and send back
  6. A recipe card for a dinner you'll cook together on video

For the reunion countdown

  1. A mini calendar to cross off the days
  2. An envelope to open the night before the flight
  3. A small travel kit for the trip to you
  4. A printed boarding pass mock-up for the next visit

Little surprises

  1. One wrapped object per day for a week
  2. A hidden note in every item
  3. A song for every item, with a QR code
  4. A coupon book (“one breakfast in bed”, “one movie of your choice”)
  5. Confetti, because why not
  6. A polaroid with a date on the back for your next photo
  7. Stickers for their laptop
  8. Something ridiculous that will make them laugh
  9. A handwritten “open when” letter
  10. Your favourite childhood snack
  11. A keychain of your city
  12. A small plush that can go in their bag

Tips

  • Check customs rules for food and liquids before sending abroad.
  • Theme the box (“winter”, “exam week”, “our first trip”) so it tells a story.
  • Send a photo of you packing it: it makes the waiting part of the gift.
Made by a long distance couple.
