30 long distance date ideas that don't feel like another video call

All guidesMagpie

After a few months apart, every evening risks becoming the same video call. A date is different: it has a plan, a beginning and something you do together. These ideas work across any distance, and several of them work across time zones too.

Do the same thing at the same time

  1. Cook the same recipe together, screens propped on the counter
  2. Order the same takeaway and eat “at the same table”
  3. Watch a film with a synced countdown and a call in the background
  4. Do a puzzle of the same picture and race each other
  5. Read the same book and meet for a two-person book club
  6. Take the same online class (painting, dancing, a language)
  7. Do a short workout together and pretend to hate it

Go out, together

  1. Walk in your cities with earphones in, describing what you see
  2. Visit a museum virtually and pick your favourite room
  3. Go to a café at the same time and swap pictures of your orders
  4. Watch the sunset in your city while the other watches sunrise
  5. Plan an imaginary trip and book nothing (yet)

Play

  1. Online board games or a two-player game
  2. A quiz about each other with real stakes
  3. Draw each other badly from memory
  4. Build a shared playlist, one song each, with a reason
  5. Rate each other's childhood photos
  6. A scavenger hunt in your homes

Slow dates (great across time zones)

  1. Exchange voice notes all day as if you were together
  2. Write a story together, one sentence each, through the day
  3. Send a postcard from your town every week
  4. Leave each other a question in the morning to answer at night
  5. Keep a shared photo diary of the same moment each day

Big occasions

  1. Surprise delivery of their favourite dessert
  2. Dress up for a formal dinner on camera
  3. Rewatch the first film you saw together
  4. Plan your next reunion, hour by hour
  5. Write each other a letter to open on the next visit
  6. Record a video tour of the places you want to show them
  7. Celebrate a made-up holiday that's only yours

Tips

  • Put it in the calendar. A date that's planned feels like a date.
  • Change the setting: different room, candles, a real outfit.
  • End with something to look forward to: the next date, or a countdown to the next visit.
Made by a long distance couple.
